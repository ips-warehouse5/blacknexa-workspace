# Resource Management Module

## Purpose
The **Resource Management** module manages verified legal guides, emergency contacts, safehouse locations, pro-bono attorneys, and community support centers surfaced in the BlackNexa mobile apps.

---

## Core Features & Functionality

### 1. Resource Directory
* **Resource Categories:**
  * `Legal Support & Counsel`: Verified civil rights attorneys, legal aid societies, and pro-bono hotlines.
  * `Emergency Contacts & Hotlines`: 24/7 crisis lines, mental health responders, and community bail funds.
  * `Safe Locations & Shelters`: Temporary safehouses and community centers with verified hours and contacts.
  * `Educational Know-Your-Rights Guides`: Step-by-step PDF cards and interactive guides for stop-and-search, housing disputes, and workplace discrimination.

### 2. Management Table & Workflows
* **Search & Location Filters:** Filter resources by city, country, jurisdiction, and legal specialty.
* **Verification Status:** `Verified`, `Pending Review`, `Outdated`.
* **Add / Edit Resource Modal:**
  * Resource Name, Category, Geographic coverage, Phone/Email, Address, Website, and Operating Hours.
* **Integrity Audit:** Automated quarterly check to ensure emergency phone numbers and links remain active.
