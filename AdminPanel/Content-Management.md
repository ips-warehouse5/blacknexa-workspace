# Content Management Module

## Purpose
The **Content Management** module governs static platform content, Terms of Service agreements, Privacy Policies, Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ), and localized community guidelines.

---

## Core Features & Functionality

### 1. Static Pages & Legal Repositories
* **Terms of Service (ToS):**
  * Versioned legal agreement manager (`v1.0`, `v1.1`, `v2.0`).
  * Tracks user consent requirements and forced re-acceptance triggers.
* **Privacy Policy & GDPR Compliance:**
  * Disclosures on data retention, encryption standards, zero-knowledge location precision, and right-to-be-forgotten erasure workflows.
* **Community Guidelines:**
  * Public-facing rulebook explaining harassment policies, prohibited violent speech, and evidence verification standards.

### 2. Help Center & FAQ Manager
* **Categorized FAQs:**
  * Submitting an Incident Safely
  * Working with Verified Advocates
  * Protecting Your Identity & Location Privacy
  * Account Security & Password Recovery
* **Inline Editor & Re-ordering:**
  * Drag-and-drop hierarchy sorting and real-time live preview.
