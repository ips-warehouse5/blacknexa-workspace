# Dashboard Overview Module

## Purpose
The **Dashboard Overview** module provides a high-level command center for operators to monitor platform activity, safety status, and incoming workloads across all BlackNexa services.

---

## Core Features & Functionality
1. **Key Performance Indicators (KPI Cards)**:
   * **Pending Moderation Queue:** Total count of flagged posts and comments requiring review.
   * **Active Incidents Under Review:** Unverified and active cases assigned to advocates.
   * **Verified Incidents:** Total credible verified reports published with active shield protection.
   * **Total Community Members & Active Advocates:** User base growth and active staff counts.
2. **Real-Time Activity Heatmap & Stream**:
   * Recent incident submissions with geographical markers.
   * Spike alerts for sudden surges in harassment or high-risk incidents.
3. **Queue Health & Turnaround Metrics**:
   * Average review resolution time (target: < 15 mins for urgent reports).
   * Flagged items vs approved/rejected ratios.
4. **Quick Action Shortcuts**:
   * Direct jump to urgent moderation items.
   * Trigger emergency area broadcasts.
