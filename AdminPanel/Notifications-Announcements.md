# Notifications & Announcements Module

## Purpose
The **Notifications & Announcements** module allows platform operators to dispatch urgent community safety broadcasts, push notifications, legal alerts, and direct policy notices to BlackNexa mobile and web users.

---

## Core Features & Functionality

### 1. Broadcast Engine
* **Regional Emergency Broadcasts:**
  * Area-targeted safety alerts sent to users within a specific radius (*e.g., active police curfew, localized protest safety notice*).
  * Overrides silent notification settings for critical high-priority safety warnings.
* **Global Announcements:**
  * Platform updates, new legal resource releases, and system maintenance alerts.
* **Direct Moderation Warnings:**
  * Automated and custom notifications sent directly to users regarding flagged posts, warnings, or verified incident milestones.

### 2. Campaign Manager & History Table
* **Campaign History Table:**
  * Title & Subject
  * Target Audience (*All Users, Specific Region, Verified Advocates Only*)
  * Delivery Channel (*Push Notification, In-App Banner, Email*)
  * Sent Timestamp
  * Delivery & Read Rate Metrics
* **Draft & Dispatch Composer Modal:**
  * Title, Body text, Call-to-Action link, Target Coordinates/Jurisdiction, and Priority level (*Standard, High, Emergency*).
